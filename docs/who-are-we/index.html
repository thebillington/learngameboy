Who are we?

< About

We are Billy Rebecchi and Luke Wood, two video game, programming and retro tech enthusiasts with a wealth of experience and drive to learn more about computers and how they work. At the end of 2018 we started getting interested in the idea of developing games for the Game Boy, partly because we both love the system and have memories of playing Game Boy games as a child, but mainly because of the incredible challenges that learning to write code for the system would pose.

In early 2019 we took part in the global game jam and alongside a team of extremely talented artists, narrative designers and musicians created Skazka in 48 hours. The game has no music, limited functionality and is incredibly buggy, but it is our Game Boy game and we couldn't be more proud.

Despite the successes of our first dive into Game Boy programming we found ourselves extremely discontented with the final product as it was very buggy and hard to keep working on, mainly due to the hacky nature of the initial code base. After several weeks of trying to push on working on the project we made the ultimate decision to scrap the game entirely and turn our attention instead to learning gbZ80 Assembly, a language that we feel is much more suitable for writing game code.

In his day job Billy is a Software Engineer working at Appoly, a mobile app and web development studio based in Leamington Spa, UK. He also runs his own retro refurb and repair service as well as being heavily involved in Computer Science Education.

Luke is currently finishing his final year project of his Degree in Electrical Engineering, building a computer from basic components at the Digital Logic Level.
